Orders & delivery

Is my food actually hot on arrival?+
Runners carry insulated delivery bags, so most orders arrive close to the temperature they left the stall at. If something arrives cold, you can flag it from your order history for a partial refund.
What if my runner is late?+
If a delivery runs more than 10 minutes past its ETA, Unift automatically credits part of your delivery fee back to your wallet — no need to ask.
Can I get delivery outside campus gates?+
Right now runners only deliver within campus grounds and nearby hostel blocks. Off-campus addresses aren't supported yet.

Payments & wallet

Can I pay with student e-wallet?+
Yes — Unift wallet credit, most student e-wallets, and cash on delivery are all accepted at checkout.
How do refunds work if I cancel?+
Orders cancelled before a stall starts cooking are refunded in full to your wallet. Once cooking has started, only the delivery fee is refundable.

Account

How do I become a runner?+
Head to the Runner sign-up page from the login menu — approval usually takes under a day once your student ID is verified.
I forgot my password — now what?+
Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page, or message live chat and support can reset it for you in under a minute.
